Trip notes, Lisbon, May

We arrived on Friday evening and took the metro from the airport to Baixa. The apartment was on the
third floor of a yellow building with green shutters, two streets up from the river. Our host, a
brunette in her forties named Ines, met us at the door with a folder of restaurant tips and a warning
about the hills.

Saturday was Belem: the monastery, the tower, and the custard tarts from the bakery with the blue tiles.
We took tram 15 back along the water and got off early to walk through Alcantara under the bridge.
Dinner was grilled sardines at a place with paper tablecloths; the bill for four was fifty-two euros.

Sunday morning we climbed to the castle before it got hot, then spent the afternoon in the LX Factory
market. Ines recommended a bookshop there that used to be a printing works. Flight home Monday at
eleven, taxi booked for seven thirty.
